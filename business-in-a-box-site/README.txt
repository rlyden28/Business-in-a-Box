BUSINESS IN A BOX - WEBSITE FOLDER

TO PUT THE SITE ONLINE (GitHub Pages, free)
1. Unzip this download.
2. Make a free account at github.com and create a new Public repository.
3. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in index.html and README.txt, and commit.
4. Settings > Pages > Branch: main, /(root) > Save. The site appears at yourusername.github.io/repository-name.

TO UPDATE THE SITE LATER
Upload the new index.html to the same repository; it replaces the old one.

WHERE TO EDIT CONTENT (open index.html in any text editor and search for these words)
- "const R ="      Local resources: names, links, descriptions
- "const IDEAS ="  Business ideas: costs, prices, licenses, steps
- "const TL ="     Timelines for each idea (in weeks)
- "const GUIDES =" Our team's full binders (JJ, Kathryn, Rachel, Tim), shown at the bottom of each kit
- "<footer>"       Footer text and "last checked" date

Check costs, fees, and contacts at least once a year.
